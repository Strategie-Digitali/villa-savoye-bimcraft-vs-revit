Goal: model Villa Savoye (Le Corbusier, 1931) as a BIM model and produce its drawings, using ONLY the BIM application tools connected in this session.

RULES
- Work autonomously: do not ask questions. Where the data sheet is not enough, make a reasonable assumption, apply it and note it down.
- Units are mm. Use the coordinates of the data sheet exactly as given.
- Everything is based on the grids: columns on grid intersections (or at the off-grid points listed), walls and slabs referenced to the grid lines.
- At the end of each phase, check your work (query the model and look at a render or a screenshot) and fix it before moving on.
- If a deliverable cannot be achieved with the available tools, say so and move on: no shortcuts outside the BIM application (for example, do not draw the sheet as an image instead of using real views).

DATA SHEET
Units mm. Side y=0 is the entrance front (ground-floor curved glazing); x increases to the right when facing the entrance.

1. Grids
- Square 5x5 grid, spacing 4750.
- Numbered grids along X: 1 x=100, 2 x=4850, 3 x=9600, 4 x=14350, 5 x=19100.
- Lettered grids along Y: A y=1250, B y=6000, C y=10750, D y=15500, E y=20250.
- Grid lines extend at least 1500 beyond the building footprint on each side.

2. Levels (top of slab)
- Ground 0
- Ground Floor +150 (slab 150)
- First Floor +3450 (slab 200, underside +3250)
- Roof Terrace +6750 (slab 200)
- Solarium Top +9450 (top of the curved walls +9550)

3. Pilotis
- Round columns, diameter 190.
- Ground floor, perimeter: every intersection on grids 1 and 5 (A to E), plus rows A and B on grids 2, 3, 4 (no column at B3).
- Off-grid ground-floor columns: (8300, 6000), (10900, 6000) either side of the entrance, (4850, 8300), (8300, 8300), (8300, 12660), (14350, 13600), (8300, 17030), (9600, 20250).

- First floor columns: A2, A3, A4, B2, B4, C5, D5, E3, plus (4850, 8300), (8300, 8300), (8300, 12660).

4. Ground floor (+150)
- Set-back volume: x 3050-16160, y ~4100-21500.
- Curved U-shaped facade wall towards the entrance: centre (9605, 10750), outer radius 6555, thickness 200, height 3250. It continues with straight segments to the rear front (y 21500, rear wall x 4750-14460).
- Curved entrance glazing on the same centre, radius ~6530, height 3100, in two segments: x 3060-8118 and x 11081-14046.
- Entrance door: glazed, on the axis, x 8155-11045, at y 4700, up to +3250.
- Rear ribbon window: x 5100-14110, from 1000 to 3100 above ground.
- Interior partitions 100 thick, height 3100.

5. First floor (+3450)
- Outer box: x 0-19200, y 0-21500, wall thickness 200, height 3800 (from +3250 to +7050). It cantilevers about 1200 beyond grids A and E.
- Ribbon windows: sill 1055 above the floor, height 1040.
  - Front (y=0): continuous, x 205-18994.
  - Rear (y=21500): continuous, x 206-18995.
  - Left (x=0): 4 segments, y 1351-5892, 6102-10643, 10853-15394, 15604-20145.
  - Right (x=19200): y 1355-5896; the rest of this side opens onto the terrace (unglazed openings at the same height).
- Roof garden / terrace: the area x ~9700-19000, y ~6300-15400 (open to the sky).
- Living room glazing onto the terrace: along y 6300, x 9701-18995, height 3090.
- Terrace planter: low wall height 400, x 11490-14450, y 6300-15400.
- Interior doors 1100 x 2100.
- Rooms: living room (along the terrace), kitchen, bedrooms, bathrooms; name each room.

6. Ramp (two side-by-side flights with a landing; repeated identically from the first floor to the roof terrace)

- Landing: x 8400-10800, y 16930-18210, at +1800.

PHASES
1. Levels and grids: levels with the names above; grids named 1-5 and A-E.
2. Structure: pilotis, slabs of every level.
3. Envelope and circulation: ground floor with the curved facade and glazing, first floor box with the ribbon windows, roof garden, ramp, stair, solarium curved walls.
4. Rooms with name and area.
5. Drawings:
   - Plans of every level, dimensioned: chain of the grid spacings on two sides, overall dimensions, main openings along the perimeter; door and window tags.
   - 2 sections: a longitudinal one along the ramp (x = 10250, from y -2000 to y 23500, looking west) and a cross section through the terrace and the living room (y = 10750, grid C, from x -2000 to x 21000, looking north).
   - 4 elevations and a 3D axonometric view from the south-west (entrance side).
   - Scale 1:100 for plans, sections and elevations.
6. A0 landscape sheet: plans, the 2 sections, the 4 elevations and the 3D view laid out in an orderly, aligned way, each view titled, title block filled in (project "Villa Savoye", sheet number, scale).
7. Export: PDF of the sheet, IFC of the model, native project file. Report the paths.
8. Finally, open the A0 sheet view in the application window so it is on screen. If your tools cannot switch the active view, just say so in the report.
